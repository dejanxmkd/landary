Standard rules

These rules apply to future Landary work.

Use Archivo everywhere

Archivo is the default font family for every future Landary page, section and component.

Keep the weight range limited

Use only 400, 500, 600 and 700. Do not introduce extra weights unless explicitly required.

Tighter tracking for large type

Display and heading styles use negative tracking. Body text stays at normal tracking for readability.

Responsive display sizes

Large display typography may scale down on smaller screens, while the hierarchy and weight remain unchanged.
